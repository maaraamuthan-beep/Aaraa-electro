Arduino Obstacle Avoiding Robot

Build a smart robot that detects obstacles and steers using a servo motor and Arduino.

What is this project?

An obstacle avoiding robot with a servo motor is an autonomous smart car that moves forward and avoids hitting objects in its path without manual remote control. It mounts an ultrasonic distance sensor on a rotating servo motor so the robot can scan left and right before choosing a clear direction.

How does it work scientifically?

The ultrasonic sensor sends out high-frequency sound pulses (ultrasonic waves) and measures the echo time taken for the waves to bounce back off an obstacle. Using the speed of sound, the Arduino calculates the exact distance to the object in front of the robot.

When an obstacle comes closer than the safe limit, the Arduino stops the motors, rotates the servo motor to scan left and right distances, compares which side has more open space, and drives the wheel motors via the motor driver to steer safely around the obstacle.
