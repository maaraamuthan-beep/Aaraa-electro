Required Components

1. Arduino Uno (with USB Cable)

Runs the Blink sketch and toggles Digital Pin 13 between HIGH (5V) and LOW (0V).

2. Standard 5mm LED

Lights up when current flows from the Anode (+) to the Cathode (−) and turns off when LOW.

3. Current-Limiting Resistor (220Ω – 1kΩ)

Protects the LED from excess current and prevents burnout when Pin 13 goes HIGH.

4. Solderless Breadboard

Lets you plug in the LED, resistor, and jumper wires cleanly without soldering.

5. Jumper Wires (Male-to-Male)

Connects Arduino Pin 13 to the resistor/LED and connects the LED cathode to GND.
