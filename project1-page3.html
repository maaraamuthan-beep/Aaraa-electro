Required Components

1. Arduino Uno (with USB Cable)

The main microcontroller brain that processes sensor signals and controls motors.

2. L298N Motor Driver Module

Controls the speed and direction of the DC gear motors.

3. IR Infrared Sensor Modules (2 Nos)

Detects the black line on the white surface using infrared light.

4. BO Gear Motors & Wheels (2 or 4 Sets)

Provides movement to the robot chassis.

5. Robot Chassis & Caster Wheel

The base frame to mount all components securely.

6. Li-ion Batteries (2x 3.7V) & Battery Holder

Powers the Arduino and motor driver module.

7. Jumper Wires & On/Off Switch

Male-to-Female and Male-to-Male wires for making connections.
